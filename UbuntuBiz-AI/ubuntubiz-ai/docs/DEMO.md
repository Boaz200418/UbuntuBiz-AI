# Five-minute capstone walkthrough

## 1. Begin with the vision (30 seconds)

“UbuntuBiz AI helps young people, artisans, farmers and creators turn experience into an opportunity. It works without registration or paid API keys. I am because we are.”

Show the native home dashboard, tagline, artwork and featured businesses. Explain that the directory and opportunities are fictional demonstration data.

## 2. Tell a founder story (90 seconds)

Open **Create My Business Story** and enter:

- Name: Rudo Moyo
- Business: Rudo Herbs
- Country: Zimbabwe
- Category: Agriculture
- Story: “I learned to grow herbs with my grandmother in our family garden. Neighbours began asking for seedlings, and I want to turn that skill into a small business.”
- Products: Fresh culinary herbs, seedlings and small kitchen-garden starter packs.
- Goal: Supply three local kitchens and run one beginner growing workshop each month.

Tap **Transform My Story**. Show the entrepreneur story and five companion drafts. Point out the original quotation and attribution. Explain that local templates add structure without inventing achievements. Show text export or the share sheet; cancel the share if no recipient is intended.

## 3. Move towards action (60 seconds)

Open **Launch**, select **Startup budget** and enter ZAR estimates: equipment 2,000; stock 1,500; marketing 500; reserve 1,000. Generate and show the 5,000 total. Then create a quotation with quantity 3 and unit price 150: the total before tax is 450. Explain that tax is not guessed and these are drafts for review.

## 4. Find community and support (60 seconds)

Open **Discover**, search “Kijani” and open the business details. Demonstrate the honest contact preview for a fictional listing. Search an unmatched phrase to show the empty state, then reset.

Open **Funding and Opportunities**. Show a sample opportunity's eligibility and preparation checklist. Emphasise that no real programme is invented and no application is submitted.

## 5. Languages and ownership (60 seconds)

Open the top-bar language icon. Preview the sample greeting in Shona, Swahili or isiZulu. Enter an unsupported sentence to demonstrate the transparent offline limitation. This is a phrasebook, not a general translation engine.

Open **Profile**, add skills and save. Restart the application to show persisted details and generated drafts. Explain that original authorship is preserved, sharing is explicit and local data can be deleted. Avoid deleting until you are finished demonstrating saved data.

Close with: “A story is a starting point. UbuntuBiz AI gives it structure, a practical next step and room to grow.”

## Optional technical extension

Show the companion Next.js founder studio, Node.js route, strict structured-output schema and PostgreSQL migration. Show Terraform's default zero-resource flags. Explain the separation between the working offline MVP and the optional future cloud integrations. Do not imply live AI, cloud sync or AWS deployment is active.
